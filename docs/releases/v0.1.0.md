# xTrain 0.1.0

First complete prototype, 2026-10-03.

**Live:** https://xtrain-pi.vercel.app · Source: https://github.com/ivannadil/xTrain · Claude artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/1dEf752dbJvqghepzFaW5X (private until shared)

## Highlights

- A four-week soccer training plan cut from twelve signup answers, with a one-line reason on every session.
- The plan is a filmstrip you can drag: a clip's width is its minutes, today sits under an orange playhead, and xTrain warns before a move stacks two hard days or lands on a team day. Every edit has Undo.
- 30 authored solo drills with scrubbable SVG pitch plans, three cues each, common mistakes, harder and easier versions.
- A session player built for a phone propped against a wall, with a three-tap log afterwards.
- Ratings that move only with logged minutes, consistency and difficulty; a scrubbable season chart; badges with real progress.
- A scripted Course AI coach that explains any session, adapts it to missing kit or time, and changes the week's load.

## Verification

23 Playwright flow tests passing on desktop and phone (1 skipped by design), 39 route captures with zero horizontal overflow, 0 design-detector findings, finish review fix list fully resolved.

## Known limits

Rule-based planner (no model), synthetic demo data, no accounts or backend, no real drill video, camera-based Coach AI is roadmap only.
