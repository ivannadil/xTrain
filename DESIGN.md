# Design System: xTrain

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Highlight Reel"**

xTrain cuts a training plan the way a skill-edit cuts a highlight reel. Every week is a filmstrip of clips whose widths are their minutes; today sits under an orange playhead; drills are letterboxed clips with a timecode in the corner; the session player is a chapter bar with a work/rest slate. The ground is a floodlit pitch at night: an eleven-step ink ramp tinted green-teal (never neutral gray), with warm floodlight white for content. The grade is teal and orange, and each has exactly one job.

Density is editorial rather than dashboard: one big decision per view (the clip, the Start button, the minute cost), supporting data in tabular mono, and secondary context in a quiet 13.5px line directly under the heading. Surfaces are flat tonal steps separated by inset hairlines; depth exists only where a frame or an overlay needs it. Motion is cut like film: navigation, tabs and filters switch instantly; eased and speed-ramped motion is spent only on authored moments (reveal of today's clip, a cue slate wiping in, the week re-cutting itself), and every animated path has a reduced-motion equivalent.

The build refuses the category default it was briefed against: no glowing green stat tiles on a navy dashboard, no chatbot bubble, no uppercase kicker labels floating above headings, no decorative accent color.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Floodlit-pitch ink: eleven green-teal tinted steps are the only surface and text tones.
- Action-only orange: orange means pressable or "now" (playhead, current chapter, current week). Nothing decorative is orange.
- Teal is Course AI's voice, every data series, focus rings, selection and the caret.
- Duration as length: on any timeline a clip's width equals its minutes; every next step carries its minute cost as a timecode.
- Archivo variable at two widths (125 wide for display, 62 condensed for captions) with Azeret Mono tabular timecodes.
- Hard cuts for navigation; eased and ramped motion only on authored moments; full `prefers-reduced-motion` path.

## Colors

A night-pitch ink ramp carrying two signal colors with single jobs, plus amber and red as state tones.

### Primary
- **Signal Orange** (`orange`): pressable primary controls (the Start session button, Build my plan, the scrubber thumb, the work slate during a session) and the playhead that marks "now" (2px bar on today's clip, the vertical line through the load wave, the current chapter fill, the active nav tick, the current-week dot, the dot of the i in the wordmark). `orange-hot` is hover, `orange-deep` is active; `orange-deep` also serves as the warn icon on the light toast, where amber is not legible.

### Secondary
- **Mint Teal** (`teal`): Course AI's voice (the sparkle badge on `teal-dim`), every data series (rating bars, radar, load wave, season chart, consistency grid), focus rings (`outline: 2px solid`, offset 2px), text selection, the caret, and the done check on a completed clip. `teal-deep` is the toast icon on light ground; `teal-dim` is the tinted ground under teal text and the rest slate.

### Tertiary
- **Caution Amber** (`amber`): the warning tone for coach cautions, partial sessions, pain notices and "Excluded from plans". Used as text or icon only, and only on `ink-1` through `ink-4`, where it clears 8:1. It is never a surface fill at full strength.
- **Pain Red** (`red`): pain and danger only (the danger button text, the red tag). Not a validation color for ordinary form errors.

### Neutral
- **Ink 0 to 10**: `ink-0` is the letterbox bar and the darkest overlay; `ink-1` is the page; `ink-2` is cards, the rail and the tab bar; `ink-3` is clip frames, secondary buttons, chips and inputs; `ink-4` is hover ground, hairlines, track backgrounds and gridlines; `ink-5` is the strong hairline, scrubber track and scrollbar thumb; `ink-6` is a surface and border tone only (the planned segment of the week bar, underline decoration), never text; `ink-7` is tertiary text and inactive nav; `ink-8` is secondary text and placeholders; `ink-9` is body text in cards, chip text and completed-chapter fill; `ink-10` is primary text, the selected chip and the toast ground.

### Named Rules
**The Action-Only Orange Rule.** Orange appears only on something you can press or on the mark that means "now". If an element is neither, it is not orange.

**The One Voice Rule.** Teal text and the teal sparkle mean Course AI is speaking. Nothing else speaks in teal; Course AI is never introduced by a label.

**The Amber Ground Rule.** Amber is text or an icon on `ink-1` to `ink-4` only. On light ground (the toast) the warn icon falls back to `orange-deep`.

**The Ink-6 Rule.** `ink-6` is a surface and border tone. It is never used for text.

**The Secondary Text Rule.** Secondary text is `ink-7` on `ink-1` and `ink-2`, and `ink-8` on `ink-3` and above (`ink-7` on `ink-3` falls below 4.5:1).

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo variable, width 125 (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Archivo variable, normal width, weight 450 (same fallback)
**Caption Font:** Archivo variable, width 62, weight 800, uppercase
**Label/Mono Font:** Azeret Mono (with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace), tabular figures

**Character:** One family stretched to its two extremes: wide, tight-set heavy display like a title card, condensed heavy caps like a burned-in slate, and a plain normal-width UI voice between them. Numbers that measure time or ratings are always mono and tabular so columns stay still while values count up.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, width 125, 30px / 40px desktop for page titles, 52px to 56px on the landing hero, 120px to 180px for the session timer, line-height 0.95, tracking -0.035em): page greetings, landing headlines, the big number.
- **Display Wide** (900, width 125, uppercase, 44px to 88px, line-height 0.92, tracking -0.02em): the landing close title card only.
- **Headline** (800, width 125, 22px / 26px desktop, line-height 0.95): section heads ("Week 3 reel", "Your ratings"), card titles at 18px to 20px, totals at 32px to 36px.
- **Title** (600, 14px to 16px): button labels, drill names, clip titles.
- **Body** (450, 15px, line-height 1.5, max 68ch via `measure`): paragraphs; 14.5px with relaxed leading inside the Course AI voice; 13px to 14px in dense card copy.
- **Context** (450, 13.5px, `ink-8`): the one line directly after a heading that carries week, theme, counts or a disclaimer. This replaces the kicker.
- **Caption** (800, width 62, uppercase, 10px to 11px, tracking 0.10em to 0.14em; 12px to 13px with `caption-stroke` on media): clip-frame slates, DOW cells, control labels ("Scrub it", "Focus", "Length"), `dt` labels, nav labels, rail tiers. Cue slates inside the session player and the landing clip scale to 16px to 26px with tracking 0.02em to 0.03em.
- **Timecode** (Azeret Mono, tabular, 11px in badges and deltas, 12.5px to 15px for rating values, 18px for stat figures, display-size for the running clock): every duration as `mm:ss`, every rating, every date label on a chart.


Type scale as shipped (px): 10, 10.5, 11, 12, 12.5, 13, 13.5, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20, 22, 26, 28, 30, 32, 36, 40, 44, 52, 56, 64, 72, 76 for Archivo; 88, 120, 160, 180 for the Azeret Mono session timers. New sizes join the scale here before they ship.

### Named Rules
**The No-Kicker Rule.** No uppercase label sits above a heading. Context goes in a 13.5px `ink-8` line after the heading.

**The Slate Rule.** Condensed uppercase captions live only inside clip frames (with `caption-stroke` over media), on DOW cells, on control labels, and on `dt` labels. Anywhere else, use title or context type.

**The Timecode Rule.** Minutes are written as a timecode (`45:00`) in Azeret Mono with tabular figures, never as "45 min" inside a clip or badge.

## Layout

The app shell is a fixed 76px icon rail on the left at the `md` breakpoint (768px) and above, with content offset by the rail; below `md` the rail becomes a 64px bottom tab bar (five tabs plus a top bar with the wordmark and profile), padded by the safe-area inset. Pages wrap in a container of 1280px (1480px for `wide` pages such as Home), with horizontal padding 16px on mobile and 32px on desktop and vertical padding 20px / 32px.

Home splits into a `1.6fr / minmax(300px, 1fr)` grid at `lg`: the clip card leads on the left, ratings and the week's load sit on the right, then the week filmstrip and a drill rail stack full width. Clip cards are 16:9 on `sm` and above and 4:3 on phones. Timelines (filmstrip, plan week, landing week) are flex rows where each session clip's `flex-grow` equals its minutes (minimum 20) and rest days are fixed 44px / 52px cells; horizontal scroll with hidden scrollbars on mobile. The load wave uses the same flex math so its peaks line up with the clips above.

Spacing follows the 4px Tailwind scale; the rhythm that recurs is 8px between inline items, 12px between related rows, 16px to 20px of card padding, 24px between sections' subparts and 32px between sections. Headings are followed by a 4px gap and the context line, then 12px to 16px before content.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal first: the page is `ink-1`, cards step up to `ink-2`, frames, chips and inputs to `ink-3`, hover to `ink-4`. Edges are inset hairlines (`inset 0 0 0 1px ink-4`, or `ink-5` for the strong variant), never outer borders. Only two shadows exist and both are authored for specific objects.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Clip** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -18px rgb(5 11 10 / 0.9), 0 2px 0 0 rgb(242 245 240 / 0.04) inset`): the `clip-frame` only; a soft drop under the frame plus a one-pixel highlight along its top edge, like a card of film on a light table.
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 56px -24px rgb(5 11 10 / 0.95)`): anything temporarily above the page: toasts, the week menu popover, the drag overlay in the plan editor.

Media inside a clip frame gets a letterbox (6% `ink-0` bars top and bottom) and a bottom gradient from `ink-0` to transparent so slates and badges read over the diagram.

### Named Rules
**The Tonal Step Rule.** Rest-state depth comes from the ink ramp and hairlines. A shadow means the object is a clip frame or is floating above the page.

**The Focus Dimming Rule.** In a `rail` on pointer devices, the hovered clip leads and its siblings step back to 55% opacity and 98.5% scale over 220ms.

## Shapes

A small radius ramp, chosen by object size: 6px for badges, tags, slates and consistency cells; 8px for buttons, chips, filmstrip clips, inputs and nav tiles; 12px for clip frames, cards and toasts; 16px reserved for the largest frames. Filters, toggles, the streak chip and the level chip are full pills. The playhead is a 2px bar, its head a small square rotated 45 degrees (the scrubber thumb and the i-dot of the wordmark share this diamond). Rating and load bars are 3px to 4px hairline tracks with rounded ends. Edges are hairlines, not strokes; corners are never sharp except on the letterbox bars.

## Components

Buttons are solid, flat and quick: no gradients, no outer borders, a 160ms ease-out press that scales to 97%.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (8px); large size 12px. Heights 36px / 44px / 56px; padding 12px / 16px / 24px; weight 600 at 13px / 14px / 16px.
- **Primary:** Signal Orange ground, `ink-0` text; hover `orange-hot`, active `orange-deep`. One per view, on the action that moves the player forward.
- **Secondary (default):** `ink-3` ground with a hairline, `ink-10` text; hover `ink-4`.
- **Ghost:** transparent, `ink-9` text; hover `ink-3` ground and `ink-10` text.
- **Danger:** transparent with a hairline, `red` text; hover 10% red wash. Reserved for destructive and pain-related actions.
- **Teal:** teal ground, `ink-0` text; hover brightness 110%. Course AI's own action (regenerate, accept its suggestion).
- **Focus / Disabled:** 2px teal outline offset 2px (global); disabled at 40% opacity with pointer events off.

### Chips
- **Style:** full pill, 40px tall (32px small), `ink-3` ground with hairline, `ink-9` text, weight 500 at 13.5px (12.5px small), optional 14px Phosphor icon.
- **State:** `aria-pressed`; selected flips to `ink-10` ground with `ink-0` text; hover `ink-4` and `ink-10` text. Used for filters and quick toggles ("Less time", "No wall"); the switch is a hard cut.

### Tags
- **Style:** 6px radius, 2px by 6px padding, condensed caption at 10.5px with 0.08em tracking. Tones: ink (`ink-4` / `ink-9`), teal (`teal-dim` / `teal`), amber (15% amber wash / `amber`), red (15% red wash / `red`), orange (15% orange wash / `orange`).

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px.
- **Background:** `ink-2` for cards on the page; `ink-3` for clip frames.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none on cards; `shadow-clip` on clip frames; `shadow-lift` on floating surfaces.
- **Border:** inset hairline (`ink-4`), strong hairline (`ink-5`) on popovers.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px to 20px; empty states 32px / 48px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `ink-3` ground with hairline, 8px radius, 12px padding, 14px `ink-10` text, `ink-8` placeholder; compact fields 32px tall with 8px horizontal padding.
- **Focus:** 2px teal outline, teal caret, teal selection.
- **Scrubber:** range input with a 4px `ink-5` track and a 14px orange diamond thumb ringed by 4px of `ink-1`.

### Navigation
- **Rail (md+):** 76px wide, `ink-2`, hairline edge. Items stack a 22px Phosphor icon (regular weight; fill when active) over a 10.5px condensed caption; inactive `ink-7`, hover `ink-9` on 60% `ink-3`, active `ink-10` on `ink-3` with a 2px orange tick on the left edge. Profile sits at the bottom as an initial in an `ink-4` circle.
- **Tab bar (below md):** 64px, 95% `ink-2` with backdrop blur, five equal columns, 10px captions, active `ink-10` with a 2px orange tick along the top edge, safe-area padding below.
- **Route changes:** hard cut (`main` is keyed by pathname; no page transition).

### Clip Frame
The unit of the world: a 12px-radius `ink-3` frame with the clip shadow, letterbox bars, a bottom `ink-0` gradient, a condensed caption slate top-left with `caption-stroke`, and a TimeBadge (`mm:ss`, Azeret Mono 11px on 80% `ink-0`) top-right. Content inside is an authored SVG pitch diagram (turf stripes at 3.5% teal, floodlight-white markings, teal run path, cone-colored markers) that plays a 2.6s speed-ramped loop on hover and freezes on the final frame otherwise.

### Filmstrip
A week as a flex row of 88px / 96px clips (64px compact), 6px gap. Each session clip grows by its minutes; rest and team days are fixed narrow cells at 60% `ink-2`. Clip header is the DOW caption (`ink-8` upcoming, `ink-7` past, orange "Now" for today) plus a status glyph (teal check done, amber check partial, `ink-7` cross skipped). Today carries a 2px orange bar along its top edge; the selected clip takes a 2px teal ring offset 2px. A load wave (teal area at 12%, 1.5px stroke at 70%) runs underneath on desktop with a 2px orange playhead through today.

### Course AI Voice
A 28px (24px compact) `teal-dim` circle with a filled teal sparkle, followed by `ink-9` text at 14.5px with relaxed leading. The sparkle is the only identifier; there is no "Course AI says" label.

### Rating Bars
Five rows: a 16px focus icon in `ink-8`, the skill name in `ink-9` at 12.5px, the delta as an 11px timecode (teal for gains, amber for losses), a 3px `ink-4` track with a teal fill that scales in from the left over 1300ms staggered 90ms per row, and the value as a 15px timecode in `ink-10`.

### Toast
`ink-10` ground with `ink-0` text, 12px radius, lift shadow, 13.5px medium text; icon `teal-deep` for Course AI and confirmations, `orange-deep` for warnings; an Undo button on a 10% `ink-0` wash. Enters over 320ms ease-out (opacity only under reduced motion), auto-dismisses at 4.2s or 9s with Undo.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put orange only on pressable controls and on the mark that means "now" (playhead, current chapter, current week, active nav tick).
- **Do** use teal for Course AI's voice, every data series, focus rings and selection, and introduce Course AI with the sparkle badge, never a label.
- **Do** use amber as text or icon on `ink-1` to `ink-4` only, and `orange-deep` for the warn icon on light ground.
- **Do** set secondary text in `ink-7` on `ink-1` and `ink-2`, and in `ink-8` on `ink-3` and above.
- **Do** follow every heading with a 13.5px `ink-8` context line when the heading needs context.
- **Do** make a clip's width equal its minutes on any timeline, and write every duration as a `mm:ss` timecode in Azeret Mono with tabular figures.
- **Do** carry the minute cost on every next step (Start button, clip, chapter, next drill).
- **Do** switch navigation, tabs, filters and chips with a hard cut, and spend eased (`ease-out`) or speed-ramped (`ease-ramp`) motion only on authored moments.
- **Do** provide a reduced-motion path for every animation (`useReducedMotion` in React, the global `prefers-reduced-motion` block in CSS) that lands on the final frame.
- **Do** separate surfaces with inset hairlines (`ink-4`, `ink-5`) and tonal steps rather than outer borders or color flips.
- **Do** keep tap targets at 44px (md buttons, chips, nav items, the 28px-tall scrubber hit area).

### Don't:
- **Don't** use orange decoratively: no orange gradients, dividers, icons, headings or washes outside a pressable control or a "now" marker.
- **Don't** use `ink-6` for text, or any neutral gray outside the eleven ink steps.
- **Don't** place an uppercase kicker or eyebrow label above a heading.
- **Don't** set condensed uppercase captions anywhere except inside clip frames, on DOW cells, on control labels and on `dt` labels.
- **Don't** fill a surface with amber at full strength or put amber text on `ink-5` and lighter.
- **Don't** animate route changes, tab switches or filter changes; those are cuts.
- **Don't** add outer shadows to cards at rest; `shadow-clip` belongs to clip frames and `shadow-lift` to floating surfaces only.
- **Don't** write minutes as "45 min" inside a clip, badge or chapter bar; the timecode form is `45:00`.
- **Don't** use a system or neutral sans for display; display is Archivo at width 125, captions at width 62.
